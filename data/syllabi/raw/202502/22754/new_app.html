Course Information

Catalog Course Description
This course is an overview of clinical applications of psychology. Topics to be covered are basic features of clinical assessment, basic features of clinical interventions, psychological testing, overview of psychotherapy models (psychodynamic, humanistic, behavioral, cognitive, group, family etc.) and research on clinical interventions. Clinical case examples as well as relevant movies and literary works will be used in order to demonstrate the workings of clinical psychology in practice.
Learning Outcomes:
1. Upon completion of this course, students will learn the history, current context and future directions of clinical psychology;
2. Students will gain an understanding about the various assessment strategies used by clinical psychologists
3. Student will demonstrate knowledge of the principles of psychotherapeutic interventions, various theories associated with these interventions
4. Students will gain knowledge of the evidence-based intervention techniques in the practice of clinical psychology
5. Students will demonstrate an understanding of the ethical and professional issues in clinical psychology
Course Objective
This course is an overview of clinical applications of psychology. Topics to be covered are basic features of clinical assessment including clinical interview, psychological testing, and observational skills. Students will complete various exercises about different assessment strategies in small groups.
There will be a testing assignment. This assignment involves the administration and scoring of a fake test. This assignment is designed to teach you about individual intelligence testing without using real test materials. As an undergraduate student, you are not ethically allowed to administer formal intelligence tests but it is important that you understand the processes involved in individual testing. It is important to emphasize that this is just an EXERCISE.
