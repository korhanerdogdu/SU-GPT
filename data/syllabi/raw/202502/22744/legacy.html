Course Information

Catalog Course Description
This course builds on "Methods and Scope of Political Analysis" course and provides a working knowledge of the total survey approach, sampling, questionnaire design and collection and analysis of survey data.
Learning Outcomes:
1. Understand the use of survey methods for scientific research on public opinion and electoral behavior.
2. Identify their sources and extent, and reduce bias and error in survey studies.
3. Learn best practices in collecting survey data.
4. Evaluate the quality of survey studies in terms of sampling, interviewing procedures, and questionnaire design.
5. Organize survey data, report descriptive statistics, and test a bivariate hypothesis.
Course Objective
Over the course of the semester, we will focus on all major stages of a survey study –i.e., research question formulation, sampling, mode of data collection, questionnaire design, pilot testing, interviewing, as well as cleaning, managing, and weighting survey data, and simple hypothesis testing employing survey data.

This course pays particular attention to practice as well. We will examine and evaluate several national and cross-national survey studies and applied social science research. Moreover, students will design their own survey instruments and acquire the necessary software and basic statistical skills commensurate with their level to examine, edit, and weight survey data, and to employ it for hypothesis testing.

Upon completion of this course, students will be equipped with the fundamental skills to design, carry out, and evaluate survey studies and have a thorough understanding of the scientific methods used to collect survey data, as well as the advantages, limitations, and all major stages of survey research.
