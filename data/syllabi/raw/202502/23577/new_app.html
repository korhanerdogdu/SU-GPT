Course Information

Catalog Course Description
This course offers an introduction to the core concepts, terminology, and financial tools used in mergers and acquisitions. It covers the M&A process from both the acquirer and target perspectives, including strategic objectives in acquisitions, deal structuring, valuation techniques, and current trends in global and local M&A markets. Designed for students aspiring to careers in investment banking or deal advisory, as well as executives seeking to deepen their understanding of M&A, the course balances practical insights from industry with essential theoretical foundations.
Learning Outcomes:
1. Understand the strategic motivations of acquirers and targets to assess how M&A decisions align with long-term value creation.
2. Learn about the key stakeholders involved in M&A transactions and discuss their roles and influence throughout the deal process.
3. Understand and apply fundamental valuation approaches to determine and justify the target company?s value in an M&A context.
4. Discuss how deal structure, payment mechanisms, and governance choices shape risk allocation and overall deal success.
5. Learn about recent local and global M&A trends to understand their implications for corporate strategy and future deal opportunities.
Course Objective
This course introduces students to the basic M&A concepts and terminology and presents the financial methods and tools commonly used in the M&A process. Interested audience for this class would be the managers of manufacturing/service firms interested in the strategic and financial aspects of the M&A decision making process, and students targeting a career in the M&A division of investment banks, private equity firms, merger arbitrage desks of hedge funds. Lectures are designed to combine theory and applications through case studies, and there will be a balanced emphasis on both. Students should have taken basic finance and accounting courses at the graduate level to follow the lectures.
