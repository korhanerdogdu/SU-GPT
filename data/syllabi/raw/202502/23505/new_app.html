Course Information

Catalog Course Description
The course will support participants' understanding of leading and participating in organizational transformation and help them apply it well. He will tell them what to do and what not to do, when and in what order. The course will specifically focus on: Defining Transformation; status and process, Organizing and aligning key components, infrastructure and resources for transformation, leading and managing stakeholders, a methodology for delegates to assess the current state of their businesses and plan for transformation, common mistakes and case studies.

Course Objective
In a complex and changing world leaders must navigate considerable tensions, uncertainty and ambiguity to deliver results. This course draws on the latest theory, practice and research to enable you to analyze, explore and reframe your understanding and experience of leadership and management in order to more confidently and creatively address the challenges faced by you and your organizations.

We begin by examining the dominant leadership canon. We then turn critically to what the canon systematically obscures: the personal and organizational costs of heroic leadership, its structural blind spots, and the conditions under which it fails. From this foundation, the course reconstructs leadership through four contemporary lenses drawn from the latest research and practice: the leader as a container of collective anxiety, as a maker of meaning in conditions of uncertainty, as an experimenter who leads through inquiry rather than prescription, and as a weaver of distributed networks and latent organizational talent. Taken together, these lenses shift leadership away from the individual hero and toward the relational, systemic, and contextual work of enabling transformation in and with others.

At the end of the course, you should have a working knowledge of the components and framework of practical leadership approaches to manage your future transformations even more effectively.
