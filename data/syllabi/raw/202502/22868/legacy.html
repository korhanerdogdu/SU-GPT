Course Information

Catalog Course Description
In this introductory-level course on entrepreneurship, students will gain a thorough understanding of the key concepts, skills, and mindset necessary for successful entrepreneurship. The course will explore how entrepreneurship drives innovation and economic growth while promoting creativity and problem-solving abilities. The course will cover disruptive technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, and the metaverse, and their impact on businesses and industries. Students will learn ideation techniques, develop business models, and acquire knowledge on marketing, sales, financial management, and the entrepreneurial ecosystem. Through interactive workshops and case studies, students will refine their startup ideas and develop essential skills for launching and managing successful ventures in today’s dynamic business landscape
Learning Outcomes:
Course Objective
Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course students should be able to;
1. Understand the fundamental concepts of entrepreneurship, including ecosystem dynamics and the roles of entrepreneurs and identify the characteristics and mindset of successful entrepreneur

2. Demonstrate the ability to recognize, evaluate, and act on emerging opportunities in dynamic environments. Understand the fundamentals of customer-centricity and its role in creating socially desirable solutions
3. Leverage digital transformation to drive advancements and competitive advantages in various industries
4. Create and refine a business models to articulate and plan entrepreneurial ventures, conduct comprehensive market research to analyze competition and identify unique opportunities.
5. Create a go-to-market strategy and effective sales plans, master branding, advertising, and digital marketing techniques to enhance market presence.
6. Plan and manage resources efficiently, prioritizing tasks and metrics to ensure effective execution. Utilize data-driven execution strategies to optimize business operations and outcomes.
7. Develop financial acumen and skills for pitching ideas to secure funding from investors or stakeholders.
8. Gain a comprehensive understanding of the entire entrepreneurial journey, including the challenges faced by first-time entrepreneurs, with a clear focus on identifying risks.
9. Preparing a business plan, pitch readiness, and alignment with real investment opportunities
