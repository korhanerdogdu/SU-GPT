Course Information

Catalog Course Description
In this Data Visualization course, students will embark on an exploration of how to effectively represent and communicate data through visual means. The course begins with foundational concepts, including principles of visual perception and design, to understand how to create intuitive and engaging visualizations. Students will learn to leverage various types of visual representations such as charts, graphs, and interactive dashboards, and will delve into advanced techniques like data mapping, multidimensional visualizations, and real-time data interaction. The course will also cover essential tools and technologies used in the field, including popular visualization libraries and software. Emphasis will be placed on both the theoretical aspects of visualization and practical skills, ensuring students can translate complex data sets into clear, actionable insights. Through hands-on projects and real-world case studies, students will gain experience in designing and implementing effective visualizations that meet the needs of diverse audiences and stakeholders.
Learning Outcomes:
1. Understand Visualization Principles: Students will be able to explain and apply fundamental principles of data visualization, including design theories, visual perception, and effective communication strategies to create clear and insightful visual representations of data.
2. Create and Interpret Visualizations: Students will develop the ability to create a variety of visualizations?such as charts, graphs, and dashboards?using industrystandard tools and libraries, and will be able to interpret and critique the effectiveness of these visualizations in conveying data insights.
3. Utilize Data Visualization Tools: Students will gain hands-on experience with popular data visualization tools and technologies, including programming libraries (e.g., D3.js, Matplotlib), and will be proficient in selecting and using the appropriate tools for different visualization tasks.
4. Design Interactive Dashboards: Students will learn to design and implement interactive dashboards that allow users to explore and analyze data dynamically, incorporating features like filtering, zooming, and real-time updates to enhance user experience and data exploration.
5. Analyze and Communicate Data Insights: Students will develop skills to analyze complex data sets and effectively communicate the resulting insights through compelling visualizations, tailoring their presentations to diverse audiences and making data-driven recommendations based on their visual analysis.
Course Objective
By the end of the course, students will be able to:
-Understand Visualization Principles.
-Create and Interpret Visualizations.
-Utilize Data Visualizations.
-Design Interactive Dashboards.
-Analyze and Communicate Data Insights.
